Kural RAG 2026 · solo

Cross-lingual retrieval over classical Tamil · Python, sentence-transformers, LaBSE, FastAPI, Next.js

  • Answers plain-English questions from 1,330 classical Tamil verses and cites the specific verses behind every claim.
  • Retrieval accuracy 44 → 97 out of 100 on a hand-labelled golden set across six independently measured changes, each verified with McNemar's exact test.
  • On the combined 233-question set: a correct verse appears in the five shown for 78%, and the first verse shown is correct for 56%. Both numbers reported together — quoting only the higher one misstates what a user sees.
  • Hand-labelled the 233-question evaluation harness before any optimisation, then validated every change against it — and declined a 15-point improvement that landed at p = 0.0534 rather than claim it.
  • Hybrid retrieval combining LaBSE dense embeddings with a from-scratch BM25 index, cross-encoder reranking over the top 50, chapter-level signal blending, and HyDE query rewriting.
  • Found and documented three data-leakage incidents in the evaluation pipeline, including 121 of 798 generated questions that were verbatim copies of the test set.
  • Structured JSONL logging with distributed request tracing across the Next.js frontend and FastAPI service, prompt-keyed persistent cache, and a circuit breaker that degrades to un-rewritten search with the degraded state surfaced to the user.
Clikk 2026 · solo

On-device malicious URL detection · Python, scikit-learn, JavaScript, Chrome MV3

  • Published on the Chrome Web Store; screens links for phishing and malware before they open, with the classifier running on-device so most URLs never leave the browser.
