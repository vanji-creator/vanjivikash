PROJECTS
  Kural RAG — Cross-Lingual Retrieval over Classical Tamil
  Python, sentence-transformers, LaBSE, FastAPI, Next.js, TypeScript
  - Answers plain-English questions from 1,330 classical Tamil verses and cites the
    specific verses behind every claim.
  - Retrieval accuracy 44 -> 97 out of 100 on a hand-labelled golden set across six
    independently measured changes, each verified with McNemar's exact test.
  - On the combined 233-question set: a correct verse appears in the five shown for
    78% of questions, and the first verse shown is correct for 56%. Both numbers are
    always reported together; quoting only the higher one misstates what a user sees.
  - Hand-labelled the 233-question evaluation harness before any optimisation, then
    validated every change against it — and declined a 15-point improvement that
    landed at p = 0.0534 rather than claim it.
  - Hybrid retrieval combining LaBSE dense embeddings with a from-scratch BM25 index,
    cross-encoder reranking over the top 50, chapter-level signal blending, and HyDE
    query rewriting to close the vocabulary gap between modern questions and
    19th-century translations.
  - Found and documented three data-leakage incidents in the evaluation pipeline,
    including 121 of 798 generated questions that were verbatim copies of the test set.
  - Structured JSONL logging with distributed request tracing across the Next.js
    frontend and FastAPI service, prompt-keyed persistent cache, and a circuit breaker
    that degrades to un-rewritten search with the degraded state surfaced to the user.
  - Generation is wired with checked citations but not yet adversarially tested.
